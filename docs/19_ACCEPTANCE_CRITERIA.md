# Acceptance Criteria

V1 is accepted only when all are true:

1. One real WhatsApp Business number is connected through official Meta Cloud API.
2. Text messages are received and replied to automatically.
3. Voice notes are downloaded through official media API, transcribed, and answered.
4. Hassaniya, French, mixed Hassaniya/French, Arabic, and English tests work at an agreed practical quality.
5. Client admins can edit approved knowledge and Hassaniya vocabulary.
6. Dynamic facts are retrieved through tools rather than hallucinated.
7. AI can be globally paused.
8. Human takeover immediately prevents further AI replies.
9. Conversation history is visible to staff.
10. Duplicate Meta webhooks do not generate duplicate answers.
11. Message/send/STT/tool failures are logged.
12. No Meta/Facebook passwords or API secrets are committed.
13. HTTPS/webhook verification/security checks are complete.
14. Authorized edit/correct/archive/delete operations include confirmations/permissions and immutable audit history where applicable.
15. Deployment survives service restart/reboot.
16. Real-world pilot test is conducted before adding WhatsApp Calling.
